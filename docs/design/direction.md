# Visual direction

## Chosen

- **Warm light background** with one **pastel colour per section** (daily watch lavender, job radar mint, server slate;
  the other tones are kept for flows that are not in this repository).
- **Hierarchy:** page and section titles in a serif (Fraunces) on a tinted header with an icon chip; content in white
  cards in a sans (Plus Jakarta Sans); meta in grey with pastel tags.
- **Buttons never read as text:** dark pills with an arrow icon (primary) or white pills with a border (secondary).
- Cards with category thumbnails, avatars with initials, coloured closing tags. Icons: inline stroke SVG, no emoji.
- Errors page: plain on purpose.
- From the references: Sentry (grouped errors, copyable detail), Better Stack (status per service).

## History

- 2026-11-10 v1 dark, Vercel-like: **rejected** (generic; titles and content looked the same).
- 2026-11-10 v2 light pastel: approved after four rounds (no history bars, plain mail summary, no resolve button).
